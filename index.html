Problem Statement & Mission

Overcoming Bavarian Demographics Through Global Talent

Landkreis Regen faces a severe structural shortage of local technical workers due to demographic aging. SunTech Werk Teisnach is transforming this challenge into an opportunity by creating an international engineering hub.

Werk Teisnach Hub

German Engineering Excellence in Bavaria

🛡️

Financial Sustainability Shield

With €10M–€15M actual annual profit (10–15% EBITDA), SunTech can re-invest €1.5M–€2M/year of corporate profits as a secondary fallback if public grant disbursements face administrative delays.

01

Shop-Floor Resistance

Solution: Paid "Technical Mentors" with salary upgrades for veteran staff. German technicians are compensated for guiding international recruits, turning friction into mutual incentive.

02

Rural Support Deficit

Solution: Company-provided housing stipends, daycare access guarantees in Landkreis Regen, and spousal employment placement assistance to ensure family stability.

03

Public Funding Strategy

Solution: Primary funding secured via EU public programs (ESF+) and Bavarian grants (Arbeitsmarktfonds Bayern) to co-finance training, mentorship, and bilingual integration.

04

Target Beneficiaries

Triple Win: Legacy workers gain burnout prevention; international recruits gain career stability; Teisnach secures long-term economic vitality.
